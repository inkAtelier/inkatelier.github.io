The Artist

Hello, I'm the hand behind Ink Atelier

I started painting the way most people start collecting things they love — slowly, and then all at once. What began as small ink studies of friends and family grew into a practice built almost entirely around watching people on the best day of their lives, and trying to hold onto it in paint before the light changes.

Today my studio splits its time between live wedding painting — setting up an easel at the edge of a reception and working while the room moves around me — and quieter commission work: portraits, illustrated stationery, and one-off pieces for people who want something made specifically for them.

Every piece leaves the studio having been touched only by hand — no prints, no templates, just brush, ink, and water.

"I don't paint what a wedding looks like — I paint what it felt like to be standing in the room."
